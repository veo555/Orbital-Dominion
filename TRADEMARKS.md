# Name and logo

The source code is MIT licensed (`LICENSE`): use it, fork it, build on it, sell what you build.

The name **Orbital Dominion**, its logo and its faction branding identify the official project. MIT does not grant rights to them. You are welcome to fork and modify, and to say "based on Orbital Dominion", but please give your fork its own name and logo so players are not misled about what is official. This is a statement of intent, not legal advice.

The game's art, sound and music are generated procedurally by the code in this repository and are covered by the same MIT licence as the code.
